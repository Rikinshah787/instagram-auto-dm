Privacy Policy

Application: rikin-auto-IG · Last updated: August 3, 2026

rikin-auto-IG ("the app", "we") is a self-hosted tool that lets the owner of an Instagram professional (Business or Creator) account automatically reply to comments with a direct message, using the official Instagram API with Instagram Login. This policy explains what data the app processes and why.

Information we process

  • Connected account details — your Instagram professional account ID and username.
  • Access token — an Instagram access token you grant during login, used to send replies on your behalf. It is stored securely on the server side and never shown in the browser.
  • Comment & message events — when someone comments on your posts or messages your account, we receive the commenter's/sender's Instagram-scoped ID, username, the text, and the related media ID. This is used only to decide whether to send your configured reply and to deliver it.
  • Automation settings — the keywords, links, and messages you configure.

How we use it

  • Solely to operate the comment-to-DM automation you set up (matching your keywords and sending your reply via the Instagram API).
  • To send a one-time private reply to a commenter and, where you enable it, a follow-up message.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it for any purpose beyond operating the service.

Storage & security

Data is stored in the operator's own infrastructure (Vercel hosting and, when configured, Upstash Redis). Access tokens are kept server-side and the management dashboard is protected by an admin token. Webhook requests are verified with a cryptographic signature.

Third parties

  • Meta / Instagram — to receive events and send messages via the official API.
  • Vercel — application hosting.
  • Upstash — optional managed storage for configuration and tokens.

Data retention

Configuration and tokens are retained while your account is connected. Short-lived processing records (e.g. handled comment IDs, pending follow checks) are kept only briefly and are automatically pruned.

Your choices & data deletion

  • Disconnect in the app dashboard to remove the stored account and access token.
  • Revoke access anytime in Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → remove rikin-auto-IG.
  • Request deletion of any remaining data by emailing the contact below; we will delete it promptly.

Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
